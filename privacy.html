Privacy Policy - Saad Content Publisher

Last updated: 2026-10-29

Saad Content Publisher ("the App") is a private tool used by its owner to schedule and publish his own videos to his own TikTok account.

Data we access

- Basic profile information of the TikTok account that the owner connects (display name, avatar, account ID), obtained through TikTok Login Kit.

- Permission to upload and publish videos to that account, obtained through the Content Posting API.

How we use it

The data is used only to publish the owner's own videos at the times the owner chooses. We do not sell, share, or disclose data to third parties. We do not run advertising or profiling.

Storage

Access tokens are stored on the owner's own server and are never shared. The owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok settings (Security & permissions > Manage app permissions), after which the App can no longer act on the account.

Children

The App is not directed at children and is only used by its owner.
